# Microsoft Store Upload Walkthrough

Walkthrough for submitting packages to Microsoft Partner Center.

Target Artifact: `dist/store/ApkBolt_0.6.3.0_x64.msix`

---

## Submission Steps

1. Sign in to Microsoft Partner Center dashboard.
2. Select your registered app identity for ApkBolt.
3. Upload `dist/store/ApkBolt_0.6.3.0_x64.msix` to the Packages step.
4. Fill in listing details, screenshots, and age ratings.
5. Submit for certification.
